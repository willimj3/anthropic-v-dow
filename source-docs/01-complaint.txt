22                                    Artificial Intelligence (AI) Models

23           58.     Claude is a versatile, industry-leading large language model (LLM) that can be

24   used in many different contexts depending on a user’s needs. Anthropic first launched Claude in

25   March 2023. The company has released several more versions of Claude since then, most

26   recently Claude Opus 4.6 and Claude Sonnet 4.6 in February 2026.
27           59.     LLMs like Claude are algorithmic systems trained on massive datasets to identify

28   patterns and associations in language, and to generate outputs and take actions that resemble

                                                      -9-                COMPLAINT FOR DECLARATORY

 1   human responses and actions. Through training, models acquire predictive power and the

 2   transformative ability to take a range of actions in a fraction of the time it would take humans to

 3   perform them.

 4          60.      When deployed through a chatbot interface, Claude can interpret and respond to a

 5   vast variety of user inputs, known as “prompts,” in an intelligent, human-like way. Depending on

 6   the nature of the user’s prompt, Claude can: process basic instructions and logical scenarios; take

 7   direction on tone and “personality” when providing outputs; write in different languages; provide

 8   outputs in a variety of programming languages; analyze large amounts of information; and

 9   provide answers to user queries, with detailed background on technical, scientific, and cultural

10   knowledge.

11          61.      Claude may also be configured with tools that enable it to behave “agentically,”

12   meaning it can take actions on behalf of a user such as retrieving information, navigating online

13   resources, writing and executing code, interacting with external services, or carrying out open-

14   ended tasks that Claude plans and adapts. In certain configurations, Claude can perform tasks

15   with minimal ongoing user input, operating with a degree of autonomy. Although this agentic

16   use of AI systems is of particular interest to some users, including governments, it also presents

17   heightened risks compared to traditional, prompt-response chatbot interactions.

18          62.      AI models like Claude are not perfect. Despite developers’ best efforts, models

19   can generate inaccurate or misguided responses, or they can “hallucinate,” confidently providing

20   incorrect information. This is in part because models generate responses by sampling from a

21   probability distribution rather than by selecting outputs pursuant to predefined rules. As a result,

22   the outputs may or may not be factually accurate, and the same model, given the same prompt

23   twice, may provide two different responses.
